I cook this by feel, there must be a right way to cook it and get the absolute best result but I haven't learned that yet. I will someday for sure.

Till then, what I do is, half cook my rice and lay them out to dry and cool. In a pan, put oil, fry onion with some whole spices, put some vegetables (pea, carrot, raddish, onion stems).
The more fine cut the vegetables the better. Then putting my rice and the sauses (soya, ketchup, etc). I mostly skip the sause, I don't remember putting them or like to have it without.

Eggs go before of after putting the rice in the pan. (again, this might not be the right way).
I don;t have high flame thin pan setup, but this is how I go about it. ( I have low flame thick pan setup!)
