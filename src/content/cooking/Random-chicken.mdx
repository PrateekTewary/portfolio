# Chicken Dehati
This may not be the exact chicken dehati, but the final output looks similar so I'll take the liberty to all it so.
My goto chicken recipe - mustard oil, whole spices fry first, then fry onions, add garlic ginger, give spices, cook till onion is brown, add chicken, wait for a while, add tomatoes, add warm water.
I have repeated this style many times, minute changes make a lot of difference in taste.
Putting the onion after the chicken will allow the onion to retain of it's shape and taste. That tastes good.
Spices can be altered, altering the taste entirely.

# Dahi Chicken

This somehow came to my mind. Maybe I saw this somewhere, doesn't seem very ingenious to claim it to be my recipie.
This has stuck with me because of its ease as well. You can store in in freezer and put it in a pan anytime and BOOM, you get decent looking chicken gravy.

Put chicken in curd, put spices (tandoori masala, garam masala, jeera, haldi, salt etc). Keep it for sometime (half hours) or freze it.
For cooking, heat little oil (not much required since curd will lose its oil on heating and that will do), fry onions, give tomato based on how sour you want it to be.
Add chicken and close the lid. Let it cook. That's it.
