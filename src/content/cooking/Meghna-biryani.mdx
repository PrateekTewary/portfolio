Another recipe from [instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcNmhuTRcDD/?mdxt=MXM0NGZyb25xbnR3MA==), this one I nailed!

Followed the recipe end to end exactly, just used chicken instead of paneer like done in the reel. It came out perfectly!

Also, I didn't do gravy and rice separate. I layered the gravy and gace dum, it was good.
Not a lot of photos I have but you have to take my word for it!
