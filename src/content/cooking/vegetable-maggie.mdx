Again, followed the basics. I always feel heavy after having plain maggie.
Eating refined white flour has its evident effects for me. Though maggie is also a very easily made and comfort food.
My mother has added this concept in my mind of adding fibre to unhealthy food.

This recipie is pretty straight forward. Boiling the noodles till half cook and draining the water.
Washing them in cold running water. Chopping vegetables you like and adding them to fry.
Adding spices as per taste and then adding the noodles and followed by warm water.
Cooking till it gets dry as per your preference.
