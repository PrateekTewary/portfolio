My view - there are only a few dishes that give competition to the non-veg dishes, this is one of them if cooked right.
I am a big fan of Tadka daal, I haven't been able to give that a try yet.
I started with mix daal though. 

Starting out with soaking different lentils over night. I like Rajma, chola more followed by moong, matar and chana(in decreasing order of likeness).
Generally I keep the ones I like in larger quantity.
Starting by frying whole spices (jeera, cloves, bay leaf, star, dalcheeni etc) followed by chopped onions (never used paste, but I have been recommended to use onion, tomato paste here).
Adding spices, ginger and garlic paste (I like to add ginger long fine cut and garlic single cloves).
Then add the spices and then the washed and clean lentils.
Fry a while and add warm water. Add Ghee/butter for better taste (can do the initial frying in that).
Garnish with coriander leaves.
