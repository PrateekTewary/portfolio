Nothing extraordinary here, just how I would make any other curry in general. 
Putting Mustard oil first, whole spices on warm oil and then the chopped vegetables (onion first, then mushroom, then tomato).
Adding spices (jeera powder, garam masala, turmeric powder, dhaniya masala, etc) and salt. 
Pouring warm water once the frying is done and all vegetables and spices are mixed thouroughly.
