## Ingredients

- 750 g potatoes, cut into evenly sized chunks
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- 1 lemon, zested and cut into wedges

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220 C / 425 F.
2. Toss the potatoes with the oil, oregano, salt, and pepper.
3. Spread them in a single layer on a baking tray.
4. Roast for 30-35 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and tender.
5. Add the lemon zest and serve with lemon wedges.
