I started out with cupcakes. I knew the basics from way before (watching my mom make cakes when we used to drive her crazy for it).
Though mom used to do it on two pans, one filled with sand (we don't have an oven), I got a air fryer from my previous company as a gift which is a life saver!

I have been making cakes in air fryer, and it works out really well. I have an oven in my fully furnished rented flat but it has spoiled every cake I have put in (I don't know who's mistake that is, mine or the oven's), it comes out chewy first and then becomes hard.
Air fryer is good. I followed the simple basics - egg flour, butter, sugar, etc. Mixing them well (I knew that the fluffyness has to do with the air trapped and that only happens when you whisk it well).
The cupcakes turned out really well.

With time I tried out more variations. This one I made as a loaf with coffee in it, also a coating of coffee and chocolate with brush to give the dark color (though people told me it looks burnt from top, it wasn't).
I made a mistake here - I made them in the glass container, glass doesn't conduct heat and thus it got really fluffy at top, but not fluffy uniformly.
Though it was not bad, it was between good and okay (my personal thought). It would have been really good if made in a steel container.

I got a perfect steel container, but it sits in my cupboard waiting for the next bake for which I haven't found the occasion.

Then I made these Jar cakes, same mistake of making them in a glass container. Did them in oven, same chewy and hard.
But surprisingly, on having one hot straight out of oven it tasted really good. Next day on bringing it to office and it getting cold, it became hard rock.
Couldn't figure out why this happened. I haven't been able to familiarise myself with oven yet.
