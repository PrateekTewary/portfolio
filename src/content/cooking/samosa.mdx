The ultimate hack of this crispy covering is ... kneading the dough with a bit of oil at first and making a hard dough. In Bengal, this is called 'Moyan' (oil first kneading). This brings the crispiness of the samosa covering (Dough science). The hard dough holds the structure and doesn't break.


The filling (as per my preference and general observation of what a good samosa constitutes of) should be of basic potato and basic spices with just the right sour/tangy taste (Aamchur powder or imli for that). This in my idea is enough. Though some additional that go along are green peas(not mentionning ginger (a bit), onion (a bit) and garlic (a bit), etc, if someone wants to add as per preference)


In my experience, it is a snack which can be very delicious while being very simple at core.
