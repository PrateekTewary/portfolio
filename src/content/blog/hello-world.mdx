## Hello world

This is my first post on this website. I wanted a small corner of the internet where I can write clearly, document what I am learning, and share ideas that are useful beyond just my own notes.

I plan to write about the things I spend most of my time thinking about: software engineering, technology, career growth, and learning. Some posts will be technical, some will be reflective, and some will simply be notes from problems I solved along the way.

A few topics I want to explore here:

- Building reliable software systems
- Lessons from working as a software engineer
- Developer tools and productivity
- Mathematics, problem solving, and clear thinking

My goal is to keep this space honest and useful. **If even one post helps someone understand something better or think more clearly, this blog will be worth it.**

Thanks for stopping by. I'm excited to keep writing and learning in public.
